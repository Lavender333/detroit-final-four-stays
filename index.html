A sense of place

History in the walls.
Detroit at the door.

Built in 1872 as the Schloss House, this three-story mansion at 234 Winder Street carries the character of Detroit’s Brush Park into its next chapter. The property was listed on the State Registry of Historic Sites in 1988.

Mona’s Brush Park story

For Mona Ross Gardner, Brush Park is personal. Born in the neighborhood, she returned in 1985 and saw historic homes in need of care. Her first restoration began on Eliot Street—the same street where she was born.

That commitment grew into the restoration of the Schloss House as the Winder Street Inn, now presented as Heritage Inn 1872. Her work connects the mansion’s history with a lasting belief in the neighborhood and the people who call it home.

Mona’s story is one of preservation, hospitality, and helping Brush Park move forward while keeping its character alive.
